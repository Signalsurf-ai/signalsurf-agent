# Author voice

Use supplied or authorized author/brand examples and explicit preferences. Extract observable habits: vocabulary, openings, directness, warmth, punctuation, emoji, sentence rhythm and endings. Do not replace those habits with a generic house style or infer the author's identity from someone else's writing.

Style examples supply voice, never facts. A sample's customers, anecdotes, metrics or personal experiences cannot become claims in the new piece unless separately supported by the current fact sources. For reposts and quoted posts, only the account's own commentary is its voice evidence.

Follow explicit user language and tone instructions within the operation's hard constraints. When no examples exist, match the intended venue and available context; do not claim to reproduce a verified personal style. Do not block a simple draft on a full brand interview. Prefer the user's sound wording over a stylistic replacement that says the same thing less directly.
