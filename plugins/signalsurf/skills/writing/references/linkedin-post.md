# LinkedIn owned post

Choose a shape supported by the material: an observation, explanation, supported opinion or real reported experience. Start with a concrete point the intended audience cares about. The opening should stand on its own before the platform's actual fold; do not invent a story, dramatic failure, client result or statistic for a hook.

Develop one useful idea with the author's evidence and perspective. Use readable paragraphs and only the structure the material needs. Avoid engagement bait, manufactured controversy, repetitive one-line revelations and Unicode pseudo-bold. A concluding question or CTA should follow from the content and user goal, not appear by default. Preserve requested links and approved formatting; never assume a reach or algorithm guarantee.
