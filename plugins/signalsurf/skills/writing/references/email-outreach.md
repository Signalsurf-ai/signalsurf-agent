# Email outreach

Lead with the recipient's situation and a supported reason this message is relevant. Connect the observation to the problem or useful offer; inserting a name or an unrelated compliment is not meaningful personalization. Use proof only when supplied, and keep the offer proportional to the evidence.

Keep the message easy to answer with one clear, low-friction ask when an ask is requested. Avoid a company introduction, feature dump, manufactured urgency, generic flattery or a meeting demand before relevance is established. Prefer short, informative subjects for new threads. Never create fake Re: or Fwd: subjects; native thread rules and authored subjects remain authoritative. Greetings and signatures follow the caller's rules.
