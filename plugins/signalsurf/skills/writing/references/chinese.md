# Chinese calibration

When the target text is Chinese, preserve the requested Traditional or Simplified script and the intended region's vocabulary. Use author examples and the conversation as evidence; do not impose Taiwanese conventions on another Chinese-speaking venue.

Prefer natural Chinese syntax over literal English marketing formulas. Remove empty grandeur, redundant connective phrases and repeated promotional or contrast constructions when they add no information. Do not translate an English banned-word list word for word. Keep deliberate author habits, quotations, names, numbers, URLs and technical identifiers intact. Punctuation and sentence length have no universal authorship threshold; do not add particles, typos or slang merely to appear human.
