# Review and rewrite

Identify specific defects before editing: unsupported claims, irrelevant background, empty praise, repeated formulas, voice drift, unnecessary length or a mismatch with the task and venue. Diagnose the actual text; a single formal word, dash, clean sentence or conventional structure is not automatically a defect.

For review, report concrete findings with short evidence and leave the text unchanged. For rewrite, repair only the requested scope and preserve supported meaning. Prefer removing or replacing filler to adding it. If the original wording is sound and more direct, keep it. Do not regularize quotations or edit outside a selected passage.

Check the result against the original facts, intent, language, voice, quotes, protected ranges and caller's output contract. Do not print diagnostics in sendable copy or replacement-only output. An AI detector score, generic self-rating or popularity metric cannot establish writing quality.
