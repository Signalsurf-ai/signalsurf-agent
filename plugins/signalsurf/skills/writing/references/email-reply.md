# Email conversation reply

Answer the actual question or respond to the latest unresolved point before adding background. Preserve agreed details and next steps from the supplied thread. Match the relationship and the contact's language; do not turn an ordinary conversation into a cold-sales pitch.

Be as brief as the decision allows, retaining necessary qualifications. Ask only the question needed to continue. Do not invent commitments, availability or promises on the author's behalf. Leave the subject, greeting, quoted history and signature to the caller's exact requirements.
