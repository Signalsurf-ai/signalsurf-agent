# Outreach follow-up

Add a supported new angle, useful detail or resource. A reminder can be plain when that is the actual request; do not manufacture novelty or pretend to have received a reply. Avoid guilt, escalating pressure and a generic "just checking in" that gives the recipient no useful reason to answer.

Only reference earlier messages, commitments or elapsed time when the supplied conversation or sequence evidence supports them. Do not invent a last-touch promise, deadline or breakup message. Follow the requested cadence and existing stop-on-reply behavior; writing guidance never changes scheduling or send conditions.
